# Docket Contractor Agreement

**Version:** v1

This agreement is between Wisdom Abioye, doing business as Docket ("Docket") and the attorney signing below ("Attorney"). It takes effect on the date of Attorney's signature.

## 1. Engagement
Docket provides AI-assisted case-preparation software for U.S. immigration filings. Attorney engages Docket as an independent service provider to support Attorney's preparation of immigration filings for Attorney's clients. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership, or joint-venture relationship between the parties.

## 2. Attorney of record
Attorney is the licensed practitioner of law and the attorney of record for every case prepared with Docket's tools. Docket does not practice law and provides no legal advice. All work product generated by Docket's software is a draft for Attorney's review.

## 3. Attorney responsibilities
Attorney is solely responsible for: (a) reviewing, correcting, and approving every output generated by Docket before it is shared with a client or filed with USCIS; (b) the client relationship, including engagement, communication, fees, and trust accounting; (c) maintaining current professional malpractice insurance; (d) compliance with the rules of professional conduct in every jurisdiction where Attorney practices; and (e) filing all submissions with USCIS or other government bodies.

## 4. Docket responsibilities
Docket will: (a) provide reasonable access to its software during the term; (b) maintain the confidentiality of beneficiary and case data per its published security practices; and (c) operate the software in good faith.

## 5. Revenue share
For each completed case prepared using Docket, Attorney will collect the gross case fee directly from the client. Within 30 days of receipt, Attorney will remit 15% of that gross fee to Docket and retain 85%. "Gross case fee" means the total professional fee billed for the case, excluding USCIS filing fees, translation fees, and other third-party pass-through costs.

## 6. Intellectual property
Docket retains all right, title, and interest in its software, models, prompts, templates, processes, and any improvements thereto. Attorney retains all right, title, and interest in client communications, client-supplied evidence, and the final filed submissions. Neither party grants the other any license beyond what is necessary to perform this agreement.

## 7. Confidentiality
Each party will treat the other's non-public information as confidential and use it only to perform this agreement. Beneficiary data is the client's confidential information, held by Attorney; Docket processes it only as Attorney's service provider and will not use it for any purpose other than providing the service to Attorney.

## 8. Term and termination
This agreement begins on signing and continues until terminated by either party on 30 days' written notice (email is sufficient). Cases in progress at the time of termination will be completed under the terms of this agreement. Sections 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, and 10 survive termination.

## 9. Disclaimer and limitation of liability
Docket's software is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind. Neither party is liable for any indirect, incidental, special, consequential, or punitive damages. Each party's total liability under this agreement is capped at the total revenue share received by Docket from Attorney in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## 10. General
This agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject and supersedes any prior understandings. It is governed by the laws of the State of New Mexico, without regard to conflict of laws. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder remains in effect. Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent, except to a successor in a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets. Notices may be sent by email to the addresses on file.
